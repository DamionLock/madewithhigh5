HIGH5 is a simple badge for content created by people, with AI. It tells your audience that a human started the work, steered it and signed it off. AI helped shape it.

Get the badge What HIGH5 means

The mark

HIGH = Human Initiative, Generatively Honed

A high five takes two hands. One is yours, one is the machine's, and yours comes first.

H

Human

A real person owns the idea, the brief and the final word.

I

Initiative

The work starts with human intent, not a blank prompt left to run.

G

Generatively

AI tools draft, explore, edit and speed up the making.

H

Honed

The person reviews, corrects and refines until it's right.

How it works

Three steps to a HIGH5 piece

You initiate

Set the purpose, audience and facts. Decide what the piece must say.

AI generates

Use AI to draft, design, summarise or translate, under your direction.

You hone and sign

Check it, fix it, make it yours. Then add the HIGH5 badge.

Use the badge

Add HIGH5 to your work

Put the badge on documents, articles, posts, slides and videos where a person led and AI helped. It's free to use.
